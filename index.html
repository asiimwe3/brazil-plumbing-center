Where is Brazil Plumbing Center located?

We are on Ruhandika Street, in the heart of Fort Portal City, western Uganda. Walk in any day or call 0786 728 924 for directions.

Why don't you show prices on the website?

Prices change with stock and supplier rates, so we quote the current price directly. Tap "Ask price on WhatsApp" on any photo or send your order list and we reply with exact prices — usually within minutes.

Do you deliver within Fort Portal?

Yes. We deliver orders within Fort Portal City at a small delivery fee depending on your location. Ask on WhatsApp 0786 728 924 and we confirm the cost instantly.

What products do you stock?

Toilets, wash hand basins, kitchen sinks, rain shower sets and mixers, PVC pipes and fittings, GI fittings, gutters, bathroom accessories like towel bars, hooks and shelves — and BX40 Digester Bio for pit latrines and septic tanks.
